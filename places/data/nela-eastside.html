Sitting across the LA River from Central LA neighborhoods like Los Feliz, Silver Lake and Elysian Park, Northeast LA is a hidden gem of the city that blends nature with a vibrant arts and food scene. Areas nestled in lush hills like Montecito Heights, Monterey Hills, and Mount Washington offer sweeping views of LA's skyline from unique angles unfamiliar to many, while the revitalized LA River with relaxed riverside trails and green spaces is a key feature of Atwater Village and Cypress Park, alongside Central LA's Frogtown. In Highland Park, Eagle Rock, and Glassell Park, you'll find one-of-a-kind restaurants, bars, and arts venues that reflect the city's creative, ever-evolving culture.

Neighbors to the south, the Eastside refers to some of LA's most historic neighborhoods directly east of Downtown LA: Lincoln Heights, Boyle Heights, El Sereno, and the unincorporated area of East Los Angeles. The Eastside is proudly shaped by a long history of diverse immigrants and today the heart of LA's Chicano culture, community, and activism. Known for vibrant murals, authentic Mexican food, historic architecture, and a strong sense of community, the Eastside is anchored by Whittier Boulevard, which transitions into the new 3,500-feet-long Sixth Street Bridge – a modern civil landmark opened in 2022 that connects the Eastside to the Arts District with panoramic views of LA's skyline.
