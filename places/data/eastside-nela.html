The Eastside of the city refers to some of LA's most historic neighborhoods just east of Downtown LA: Boyle Heights, Lincoln Heights, El Sereno, and the unincorporated area of East Los Angeles. The region is proudly shaped by generations of diverse immigrants and long-standing Chicano culture, community, and activism. Known for vibrant murals, authentic Mexican food, historic architecture, and a strong sense of community, the Eastside is anchored by Whittier Boulevard, which transitions into the new 3,500-feet-long Sixth Street Bridge – a modern civil landmark opened in 2022 that connects the Eastside to LA's Arts District with panoramic views of downtown's skyline.

North of the Eastside and sitting across the LA River from Central LA neighborhoods like Silver Lake, Los Feliz, Frogtown, and Elysian Park, Northeast LA has been a hidden gem of the city for years – blending lush nature, unique city views, and an evolving food scene into a laid-back part of the city. The LA River is a defining feature of Atwater Village and Cypress Park, offering relaxing green spaces and revitalized riverside trails, while Mount Washington and Montecito Heights sit above it all with sweeping views from their hillside trails. Standing out for their food scenes, Highland Park, Eagle Rock, and Glassell Park mix longtime favorites with new spots reflecting the area's creativity.
