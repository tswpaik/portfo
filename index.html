Collaboration Process

Expertise

Collaboration Process

Collaborate with a dedicated partner – every step of the way, from start to finish.

1. Consultation

During our initial calls, we'll discuss your project needs, wishlist, and more. I will help flesh out your ideas, build an action plan, and go over potential design solutions we can explore for your need.

2. Design Proposal

I will propose specific designs using wireframes and/or prototypes, collect your input, user test where possible, and iterate as necessary. We will work together to align and arrive at the final design to build.

3. Development

Once we have documented timelines and requirements, I will architect and build the product accordingly, with status checkpoints scheduled ahead of time to show development progress and solicit input.

4. Delivery & Support

Once development is complete, I will showcase the final product and gain your confirmation and sign-off. As an established partner, I'll be available for support and consultation on future needs you may have.
